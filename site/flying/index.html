Flight history

More than hours in a logbook.

The interesting part is what happened along the way: instruction, business ownership, experimental aircraft, engineering evaluations, simulator development, formation work and long ferry flights.

2007–2009

Learning to fly

Private pilot certificate at 17, followed by commercial and instructor ratings in 2009, including CFI, CFII and MEI.

2009–2011

Murfreesboro Aviation

I started with one airplane and grew the school to seven aircraft and five employees. Instruction and operations during that period accounted for roughly 3,000 flight hours.

2012–2018

Mission, experimental and engineering flying

Around 2012 I hauled supplies to Haiti. I also supported flight testing of an electric Long-EZ, flew homebuilt and experimental aircraft, and connected Red Bull Air Race simulation work with real pilot feedback.

2018–2022

Aircraft development and airshows

BlackFly development, physical simulator evaluation, aircraft ferrying, GoEZ Aerobatics support and increasingly complex professional simulator work.

2023–present

Professional flying becomes the main work

Cirrus instruction and operations, SF50 type rating, PA-31 and PA-46T flying, TL Sport Aircraft demonstration and test flying, formation and photo-ship missions, and live-flight HMARD testing.

2026

Across the Atlantic and across the country

An SR22 crossing of the Atlantic, DA62 flying, coast-to-coast ferry work in a Navion and bush plane, and photo-ship flying alongside a Lockheed Constellation.

What the flying includes

Instruction, operations, testing and airshows.

Commercial ASEL, AMEL and ASES; instrument airplane; CFI, CFII and MEI; and an SF50 type rating earned in January 2024.
