A life beyond the résumé

25,000 km changed the view.

After losing my father and selling the flight school I had built, I left Tennessee on a motorcycle and headed east. The road crossed oceans, borders, deserts, mountain passes and eventually brought me home with a very different idea of what matters.

That journey became The $500 Bet and How I Met My Father.
