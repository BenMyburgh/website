The $500 Bet and How I Met My Father

After my father died and I sold the flight school I had built, I left Tennessee on a motorcycle and headed east. The route crossed Europe, the Alps, Russia and Mongolia before finally bringing me back home.

The ride included dangerous roads, crashes, breakdowns, improvised repairs and strangers whose generosity changed the entire journey. This book is the story that came back with me.
