Aircraft engineering · Simulation · Flight test

One person across the airplane, model and test.

I help aircraft teams connect mechanical design, professional simulation and real pilot feedback—especially where a small development program needs somebody who can work across disciplines.

The overlap is the advantage.

Aircraft development loses time when engineering, simulation and flight operations cannot translate for one another. My background spans mechanical design, prototype fabrication, X-Plane professional simulation, embedded systems and 4,500+ hours in the cockpit.

I am most useful on practical development programs where the model needs to match the machine, the test plan needs to answer an engineering question and pilot feedback needs to become an actionable change.
