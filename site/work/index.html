Aircraft · Simulators · Products · Experiments

Projects, airplanes and things worth trying.

My work has moved between aircraft structures, production tooling, flight simulators, mixed reality, consumer products and a long list of one-off problems that needed a practical answer.

The work never fit one box.

I started with CAD, welding, motorcycles and airplanes. Since then I have worked on production cars, experimental aircraft, air-race modifications, simulators, manufacturing systems and live-flight augmented reality.

The common thread is straightforward: understand the problem, model what matters, build something real and find out what it does.

2023–present

HMARD

I designed, integrated and flight-tested a helmet-mounted mixed-reality system combining aircraft data, head tracking, Unity and live-flight evaluation.

Professional simulation

X-Plane and complete cockpits

Aircraft models, professional support, Sim Your Plane kits and complete training systems ranging from an MD500 aerial-saw simulator to full-force-feedback and full-motion cockpits.

2018–2020

BlackFly at Opener

As Mechanical Design Engineering Manager, I led mechanical design and built aircraft structures, production tooling, gauges, jigs and physical simulators for what is now Pivotal.

2015–2016

Stratolaunch at Scaled Composites

I supported center-wing design and construction through engineering calculations, drill-template design, composite layups and hands-on aircraft assembly.

2016–2019

Red Bull Air Race

Aircraft modeling, track optimization, aerodynamic and mechanical changes, winglets, custom data tools, G-meters, simulator development and flight-test feedback.

2014–2018

Nissan Leaf production engineering

I led quality work for the Leaf, designed inspection tooling and helped introduce smart cameras, 3D printing, scanning and wearable technology into plant operations.
